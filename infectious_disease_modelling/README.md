# Recommended Reading on Introductory Infectious Disease Modelling

- Tooling-up for infectious disease transmission modelling. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.epidem.2020.100395
- The SEIRS model for infectious disease dynamics. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-020-0856-2
- Modeling infectious epidemics. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-020-0822-z
- Uncertainty and the management of epidemics. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-020-0943-4
- An introduction to compartmental modeling for the budding infectious disease modeler. https://doi.org/10.1080/23737867.2018.1509026
- Mathematical modelling and prediction in infectious disease epidemiology. https://doi.org/10.1111/1469-0691.12308
- Mathematical Models of Transmission and Control of Infectious Agents. https://www.researchgate.net/publication/340922578_Mathematical_Models_of_Transmission_and_Control_of_Infectious_Agents
- Best practice assessment of disease modelling for infectious disease outbreaks. https://doi.org/10.1017/S095026881800119X
- A Practical Introduction to Mechanistic Modeling of Disease Transmission in Veterinary Science. https://doi.org/10.3389/fvets.2020.546651
- A User's Guide to Infectious Disease Modelling. https://prism.edu.au/publications/prism-modeling-guideline/
- Mathematical Modeling of the Transmission and Control of Foodborne Pathogens and Antimicrobial Resistance at Preharvest. https://doi.org/10.1089/fpd.2010.0643
- Mathematical models of infectious disease transmission. https://doi.org/10.1038/nrmicro1845
- Guidance on use of modelling for policy responses to COVID-19. https://www.hitap.net/en/documents/178601
- How Modelling Can Enhance the Analysis of Imperfect Epidemic Data. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pt.2019.01.009
